# FundTrace

An entirely offline, single-user Labour Society funds-tracking application. It opens directly to the workspace; no login system, account setup or remote service is used. The owner's offline/no-login amendment supersedes those parts of PRD v1.0.

Phases 1–3 establish Societies and reusable masters, Government receipts, approved claims, instalments, labour batches, receipt funding and reasoned reversals. Stable IDs and local change history provide receipt-to-payment traceability. Financial accuracy and separating cash from tax credits outrank convenience. Change history cannot attribute actions to authenticated individuals.

The owner approved a restrained utilitarian finance interface for accountants/management working primarily at a desk, with responsive local-browser views. No real financial dataset or brand assets were supplied; never invent balances or claims. Final distribution, backup and restore must work without internet access.
