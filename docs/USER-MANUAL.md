## 1. What Is FundTrace & Important Things to Know

**FundTrace** is a digital register book designed specifically for **Labour Co-operative Societies**. When your Society receives money from a Government Department (such as PWD, MHADA, or a Municipal Corporation) for a project or work order, FundTrace helps you track every single rupee from the day it enters your bank account to the day it is paid out to **suppliers, sub-contractors, labour contractors, and daily-wage workers**.

### Five Reassuring Facts Before You Begin

1. **Works 100% Without Internet:** You do **not** need Wi-Fi, broadband, or mobile data to use FundTrace. Everything runs inside your own computer.
2. **No Passwords or Logins to Forget:** FundTrace opens straight to your work screen. There are no usernames, passwords, or online accounts required.
3. **Your Data Stays on Your Computer:** None of your Society's financial data is ever sent to the internet or any cloud server.
4. **Records Only — It Never Touches Your Real Bank Account:** FundTrace is a **record-keeping book**, not a banking app. Clicking "Approve" or "Post" in FundTrace **does not transfer real money** from your bank. You first receive or pay money through your bank as usual, and then record the details and attach proof in FundTrace.
5. **Nothing Can Be Deleted by Accident:** Once you finalize ("Post") a receipt or payment, FundTrace locks it so nobody can accidentally erase or overwrite it. If you ever make a mistake, you can record a proper **Reversal** and **Corrected Entry**, just like a real accountant's ledger.

## 2. Installing, Opening & Closing FundTrace

There are two ways you may have received FundTrace. Most customers use **Option A (the Windows Desktop App)**.

### Option A: Windows Desktop App (Recommended)

#### How to Install (One-Time Only)
1. Download or copy the installer file named **`FundTrace-Setup-2.1.0.exe`** onto your Windows computer (you can even bring it on a USB pen drive).
2. Double-click **`FundTrace-Setup-2.1.0.exe`**.
3. *Note:* If Windows shows a blue box saying *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info** and then click **Run anyway**. (This message appears only because the installer is an independent offline program.)
4. Follow the simple on-screen steps (`Next → Install → Finish`). A **FundTrace** shortcut icon will appear on your Desktop and in your Start Menu.

#### How to Open & Close Every Day
- **To Open:** Double-click the **FundTrace** icon on your Desktop. It opens in its own window.
- **To Close:** Simply click the **`X`** button at the top-right corner of the window (or click **FundTrace → Exit** in the top-left menu). Your work is saved automatically on your computer.

---

### Option B: Portable Folder Version (Legacy Edition)

If you are using the older ZIP/folder version instead of the Desktop installer:
1. Make sure the `FundTrace` folder is extracted onto your computer's hard drive (for example, `C:\FundTrace`). Do **not** run it directly from inside a ZIP file or a OneDrive folder.
2. Double-click **`Start FundTrace.cmd`**. A black command window will open. **Keep this black window open while you work.**
3. Open your web browser (Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome) and go to **`http://127.0.0.1:3000`**.
4. When finished working, click inside the black window and press **`Ctrl + C`** on your keyboard to close FundTrace safely.

## 5. Step 1: One-Time Setup (Master Records) — Start Here!

Before you can enter any receipts or payments, you need to set up your basic lists (**Master records**) once. Follow this exact order from **1 to 5**:

```
1. Society  ──►  2. Department  ──►  3. Bank Account  ──►  4. Project / Work Order  ──►  5. Beneficiaries
```

### 5.1 Create Your Society (`Societies → Add Society`)
Click **Societies** in the left menu, then click **Add Society**.
- **Society name *(Required)*:** Full registered name of your Labour Co-operative Society.
- **Society code *(Required)*:** A short 2 to 8 character code using letters or numbers (for example, `SHRAM01`).
  - *Important:* Check the spelling carefully! Once saved, the **Society code cannot be changed** because it is used to stamp every Payment ID (such as `LSP-SHRAM01-26-27-000001`).
- **Registration number & GSTIN:** Optional registration details. If you enter a GSTIN, it must be a valid 15-character GST number (for example, `27AAAAA0000A1Z5`).
- **GST applicable & GST rate (%):** Choose `Yes` or `No`, and enter the percentage if applicable.
- **Working percentage (%):** Your Society's default working percentage (between `0` and `100`). When you create a Project later, this percentage will fill in automatically.
- **Effective from *(Required)*:** Pick the date from which these settings apply.
- **Contacts & Address:** Optional Chairman name/phone, Security contact name/phone, Address, and Notes.
- Click **Create record**.

### 5.2 Add Government Departments (`Departments → Add department`)
Click **Departments**, then click **Add department**.
- **Department name *(Required)*:** Name of the department (for example, *Public Works Department*).
- **Department type:** Choose `PWD`, `MHADA`, `Municipal authority`, or `Other`.
- **Division, Sub-division, Contact person, Phone, Email, Office address:** Fill in whichever details you have for your records.
- Click **Create record**.

### 5.3 Register Your Bank Accounts (`Bank accounts → Add bank account`)
Click **Bank accounts**, then click **Add bank account**.
- **Account nickname *(Required)*:** An easy name to recognize this account (for example, *SBI Main Current Account*).
- **Society *(Required)*:** Select the Society that owns this bank account.
- **Bank name *(Required)* & Branch:** Name of the bank and branch.
- **Account number *(Required)*:** Enter 6 to 24 digits (numbers only).
- **IFSC *(Required)*:** Enter the 11-character bank IFSC code (4 letters, then `0`, then 6 letters/digits, such as `SBIN0001234`).
- **Account type *(Required)*:** Choose `Current`, `Savings`, or `Other`.
- **Opening bank balance (INR) *(Required)*:** Enter the actual balance in this bank account on the date you start using FundTrace (enter `0` if starting fresh).
- **Opening balance date *(Required)*:** The date of that opening balance.
- Click **Create record**.

> **Very Important Tip for Bank Accounts:**
> Double-check your **Opening bank balance**, **Opening balance date**, and **Account number** before recording any transactions!
> - You cannot record any receipt or payment dated **earlier** than the Opening balance date.
> - As soon as you post your first transaction or bank statement on this account, those three fields are **permanently locked** so your historical bank book remains tamper-proof.

### 5.4 Add Projects & Work Orders (`Projects & work orders → Add project`)
Click **Projects & work orders**, then click **Add project**.
- **Project name *(Required)*:** Clear name of the work or site.
- **Society & Department *(Required)*:** Select the Society and the Government Department that issued the work order.
- **Work order number & Work order date *(Required)*:** The official work order reference and date.
- **Approved contract value (INR) *(Required)*:** Total contract value in rupees.
- **Project status *(Required)*:** Choose `Draft`, `In Progress`, `Completed`, `On Hold`, or `Closed`.
  - *Note:* If a project is marked **`Closed`**, FundTrace will not allow new claims or outward payments on it. Use `In Progress` or `Completed` while payments are still being settled.
- **Start date & Expected end date:** Optional dates (if you enter both, Expected end date must be on or after Start date).
-Click **Create record**.

### 5.5 Add Beneficiaries — People & Firms You Pay (`Beneficiaries → Add beneficiary`)
Click **Beneficiaries**, then click **Add beneficiary**.
- **Beneficiary name *(Required)*:** Full name of the supplier, contractor, or worker.
- **Society *(Required)*:** Select the Society.
- **Category *(Required)*:** Choose carefully! Your choice here decides what bill details are asked when you create a Claim later:
  - `Supplier` (material vendors, hardware stores, etc.)
  - `Sub-contractor` (firms or individuals doing certified piece-work)
  - `Labour contractor` (mukadams / contractors supplying labour groups)
  - `Individual labourer` (daily-wage or individual workers)
- **Phone, PAN, GSTIN, Address:** Optional contact and tax details. (If entered, PAN must be 10 characters like `ABCDE1234F`, and GSTIN must be 15 characters.)
- **Bank beneficiary name, Account number, IFSC:** Either fill in **all three** of these bank fields, or leave **all three empty** (if you don't have their bank details yet).
- **Shared bank account justification:** Normally, each beneficiary has their own bank account. If two family members or workers share the same bank account number in the same Society, FundTrace will ask you to type a short explanation here (at least 10 characters, such as *"Husband and wife joint family bank account"*).
- Click **Create record**.

### How to View Masked Details or Edit a Master Record Later
- **Viewing Masked Details:** For privacy on screen, bank account numbers show only the last 4 digits (`•••• 1234`), and phone numbers, PAN, GSTIN, and addresses show as `Restricted`. To see the full details, open any master record and click the **Reveal masked details** button.
- **Editing a Record:** Open the record and click **Edit record**. Whenever you edit an existing record, you must fill in the **Reason for change** box at the bottom so the audit log records why it was updated.
- **Duplicate Names:** If two different people or projects happen to have the exact same name, FundTrace will ask you to fill in the **Duplicate-name justification** box (at least 10 characters) before saving.
- **Deactivating Old Records:** Master records are never deleted. If a beneficiary or bank account is no longer used, edit the record, change **Status** from `Active` to `Inactive`, enter a reason, and save.

## 6. Step 2: Recording Money Received from the Government

Whenever the Government Department deposits money into your Society's bank account for a project, record it under **Government receipts**.

Recording a receipt happens in **3 easy steps**:
1. **Save a Draft** → 2. **Attach Bank Proof** → 3. **Confirm & Post**

### 6.1 Step A: Save a Receipt Draft
1. Click **Government receipts** in the left menu, then click **Record receipt**.
2. **Society *(Required)*:** Select your Society.
3. **Department, Project / work order, Receiving bank account:** Select the matching Department, Project, and Bank Account. *(If you don't know the project yet, you can save it as a draft now and select it before posting.)*
4. **Bill type & Bill number:** Choose `RA Bill`, `Final Bill`, or `Other`, and enter the bill number.
5. **Bank credit date & Value date:** Enter the date the money was credited to your bank account and the value date. *(The credit date must be on or after the bank account's opening balance date.)*
6. **Credited amount (INR):** Enter the **exact net rupee amount that actually entered your bank account**.
   - *Important:* Only this **Credited amount** adds cash to your Bank Book and project funds!
7. **Payment mode:** Choose `NEFT`, `RTGS`, `IMPS`, `UPI`, `Cheque`, or `Other`.
   - For `NEFT`, `RTGS`, `IMPS`, or `UPI`: fill in **UTR / bank reference**.
   - For `Cheque`: fill in **Cheque number**.
   - For `Other`: fill in **Narration**.
8. **Gross bill amount, GST deduction, Tax credit (INR):** These three boxes are for your information only (from your bill advice). Entering numbers here **never** adds extra cash to your bank balance.
9. Click **Save draft**.

> **Note:** While a receipt is in **Draft** status, it does not have a Payment ID yet and does not add any money to your bank balance. You can click **Edit draft** as many times as you like to fix any typos before posting.

### 6.2 Step B: Attach Bank Proof (Evidence)
On the saved Receipt Draft page, look at the **Bank evidence** section:
1. Click **Choose File** under *Evidence file (required)* and pick your bank proof from your computer (for example, a PDF bank statement, passbook scan, or credit screenshot).
   - **Allowed file types:** `.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.jpeg`
   - **Maximum file size:** Up to **2 MB** per file (you can attach up to **5 files** per receipt).
2. Click **Attach evidence**. You will see your attached file listed above the button.

### 6.3 Step C: Review and Post the Receipt
Once all details and at least one evidence file are attached, look at the **Review and post** section at the bottom of the receipt page:
1. **If FundTrace detects a Possible Duplicate:** If another posted receipt on the same bank account already has the same UTR/reference, same cheque number, or the exact same amount on the same date, a yellow warning box will show links to those receipts.
   - If you entered it twice by mistake, **stop** and do not post it.
   - If it is genuinely a separate bank deposit, tick *"I reviewed the matches and confirm this is a separate bank credit"* and type an explanation of at least 10 characters in **Duplicate override reason**.
2. Tick the box: **"I confirm this amount was credited to the selected bank account (required)"**.
3. Click **Post confirmed receipt**.

**What happens when you Post:**
- FundTrace assigns a permanent, unique **Payment ID** in the format `LSP-[Society Code]-[Financial Year]-[Number]` (for example, `LSP-SHRAM01-26-27-000001`).
- The credited amount is immediately added to your **Bank book** and becomes **Available for allocation** so you can pay beneficiaries on that project.
- The receipt and its evidence are now **locked** against editing.

## 7. Step 3: Recording Bills, Work Claims & Wage Sheets

Before you record an outward payment to a supplier, contractor, or worker, you first record their bill or wage sheet under **Claims & instalments**.

**Why?** A supplier or contractor might give you a bill for ₹1,00,000, and you might pay them ₹40,000 today and ₹60,000 next month. By creating a **Claim** first, FundTrace automatically tracks how much of that bill has been paid (`Part paid` vs. `Fully paid`) and prevents anyone from accidentally overpaying a bill!

### 7.1 Create a Claim Draft (`Claims & instalments → New claim`)
1. Click **Claims & instalments** in the left menu, then click **New claim**.
2. Select the **Society**, **Project**, and **Beneficiary**.
3. Enter the **Invoice / claim reference** *(Required)* (such as the bill number, RA bill number, or muster roll number).
   - *Note:* Each claim for the same beneficiary must have a unique reference. If you are making a second payment on the same bill, do **not** create a new claim—simply pay another instalment against the existing approved claim!
4. **Fill in the Category Details** (required before approving the claim):

| If the Beneficiary Category Is... | You Must Fill In These Fields: |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Supplier** | **Invoice date** |
| **Sub-contractor** | **Material / certified work description** AND **Attendance / certification reference** |
| **Labour contractor** | **Work period start**, **Work period end**, AND **Attendance / certification reference** |
| **Individual labourer** | **Work period start**, **Work period end**, **Attendance / certification reference**, **Days / quantity**, AND **Rate (INR)** |

5. **Enter the Amounts:**
   - **Gross claim including GST (INR) *(Required)*:** The total bill amount (including GST if any).
   - **GST included in gross (INR):** How much GST is already inside that gross amount (for information only; it is **not** added again).
   - **TDS deduction (INR):** Any tax deducted by your Society from this bill.
   - **Retention (INR):** Any security deposit / retention money held back from this bill.
   - **Other deduction (INR):** Any other deduction.
   - *How Net Payable is calculated:*
     $$\text{Net Payable} = \text{Gross} - \text{TDS} - \text{Retention} - \text{Other Deduction}$$
6. Click **Save claim draft**.

### 7.2 Approve the Claim
1. On the saved Claim page, check that the **Net payable** amount and details are right. (If you need to change anything, click **Edit draft** at the top.)
2. Scroll down to **Review and confirm**.
3. In the **Action** dropdown, keep **`Approve claim`** selected.
4. Tick **"I reviewed this record and confirm the selected action (required)"**.
5. Click **Confirm action**.

Once approved, the claim's status changes to **Approved** (`Unpaid`), its financial terms are locked, and it is ready to be paid in one or more instalments!

## 8. Step 4: Recording Outward Payments (Single & Labour Batches)

Once you have:
1. At least one **Posted Government Receipt** with available balance on your Project & Bank Account, and
2. At least one **Approved Claim** with pending balance on the same Project,

you can record an **Outward payment**.

### The 3 Golden Rules of Outward Payments
- **Rule 1 (Same Project & Bank):** A payment can only use money from Government Receipts that belong to the **exact same Society, same Project, and same Bank Account**.
- **Rule 2 (Totals Must Match 100%):** The total money distributed to claims (**Claim distributions**) must equal the exact total money taken from receipts (**Receipt funding**).
- **Rule 3 (Dates & Bank Balance):** The **Bank debit date** cannot be earlier than the funding receipt's credit date, and cannot cause your bank account balance to drop below ₹0 on that date or any later date.

---

### 8.1 Creating a Payment Draft (`Outward payments → New payment`)
1. Click **Outward payments** in the left menu, then click **New payment**.
2. Select the **Society**, **Project**, and **Paying bank account**.
3. Enter the **Bank debit date** (the date the money left your bank account) and **Value date**.
4. Choose the **Payment mode**:
   - For `NEFT`, `RTGS`, `IMPS`, or `UPI`: enter the **UTR / bank reference**.
   - For `Cheque`: enter the **Cheque number**.
   - For `Cash` or `Other`: enter **Payment remarks** explaining the bank withdrawal/debit proof.
5. **Paying Multiple Labourers at Once? (`Labour batch`):**
   - If you are paying **one** claim (even if split across multiple receipts), leave **Labour batch** unticked.
   - If your bank debited a single lump sum to pay **multiple workers or labour contractors** together, tick the **Labour batch** checkbox! This lets you add up to 100 approved `Individual labourer` or `Labour contractor` claims in one payment while recording **only one combined debit** in your Bank Book.
6. **Add Claim Distributions (Who Is Getting Paid):**
   - Under **Claim distributions**, select the approved **Claim** from the dropdown (it shows the beneficiary name and how much is still pending).
   - Enter the **Amount (INR)** being paid toward this claim in this instalment.
   - *(For a Labour batch, click **Add claim row** to add each worker's claim and amount.)*
7. **Add Receipt Funding (Which Government Receipt Is Paying for This):**
   - Under **Receipt funding**, select a posted **Receipt** (it shows the Payment ID and how much money is currently available).
   - Enter the **Amount (INR)** taken from this receipt.
   - *Tip:* If one Government receipt does not have enough balance left to cover the whole payment, click **Add receipt row** to split the funding across two or more receipts from the same project and bank!
   - Make sure the sum of **Claim distributions** equals the sum of **Receipt funding**.
8. Click **Save payment draft**.

---

### 8.2 Attaching Payment Proof (Evidence)
On the saved Payment Draft page, scroll to **Payment evidence**:
1. Click **Choose File** and select your supporting proof (such as the signed payment voucher, bank transfer advice, or stamped muster roll in `.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.jpeg` format, up to 2 MB).
2. Click **Attach payment evidence**. (You can attach up to 5 files.)

---

### 8.3 Submitting, Approving & Posting the Payment
Because FundTrace is a careful financial register, every outward payment goes through three quick confirmation steps under **Review and confirm** at the bottom of the page:

1. **Step 1 — Submit for Review:**
   - Select **`Submit for review`** in the Action box → tick **"I reviewed this record and confirm the selected action"** → click **Confirm action**. (Status becomes `Submitted`.)
2. **Step 2 — Approve Payment:**
   - Select **`Approve payment`** in the Action box → tick the confirmation box → click **Confirm action**. (Status becomes `Approved`.)
3. **Step 3 — Post Confirmed Bank Debit:**
   - Select **`Post confirmed bank debit`** in the Action box → tick the confirmation box → click **Confirm action**.
   - *(Note: If you already posted another payment for the exact same rupee amount on the exact same date from the same bank account, FundTrace will ask you to tick **"This is a separate debit if the amount and date match another payment"** and enter a 10+ character reason in the **Reason** box so you never post a duplicate debit by accident.)*

**What happens when you Post the Payment:**
- FundTrace assigns a permanent outward transaction ID (such as `OUT-26-27-000001`).
- The amount is deducted from your **Bank book** on the payment date.
- The **Available balance** on the linked Government receipt(s) goes down.
- The linked Claim(s) automatically update to show the new paid amount (`Part paid` or `Fully paid`).

## 9. Step 5: Fixing Mistakes Safely (Reversals & Corrections)

In proper accounting, finalized ledger entries are never erased with an eraser—instead, if a transfer bounces or an entry had a mistake, you record a **Reversal** and then create a **Corrected Entry**. Here is how to fix any mistake in FundTrace:

### 9.1 Before a Record Is Posted (`Draft`, `Submitted`, or `Approved`)
- **Editing a Draft Receipt, Claim, or Payment:** Open it and click **Edit draft** at the top-right.
- **Changing a `Submitted` or `Approved` Payment Before Posting:**
  - Scroll to **Review and confirm**, choose **`Return to draft`**, type a reason (at least 10 characters, e.g., *"Need to change payment amount and attach updated voucher"*), tick the confirmation box, and click **Confirm action**.
  - Now it is back in `Draft` status and you can click **Edit draft** or attach more files!
- **Cancelling an Unpaid Claim or Unposted Payment:**
  - If a claim has ₹0 paid against it, or a payment has not been posted yet, choose **`Cancel claim`** or **`Cancel payment`** (or **`Mark failed (no bank debit)`**), enter a 10+ character reason, tick confirmation, and click **Confirm action**. Since it was never posted, your bank balance is unaffected.

### 9.2 Reversing a Posted Outward Payment (e.g., Bank Transfer Returned/Bounced or Wrong Entry)
If a payment was already **Posted**, you cannot edit or delete it directly. Instead:
1. Open the posted payment under **Outward payments**.
2. Scroll down to the **Reverse posted payment** section.
3. Fill in:
   - **Reversal date *(Required)*:** The date the payment was returned/reversed (must be on or after the original payment date).
   - **Bank reversal evidence reference *(Required)*:** The bank return reference or memo number.
   - **Reversal reason *(Required)*:** Explain clearly why it is being reversed (at least 10 characters).
   - **Outcome *(Required)*:** Choose **`Reversed`** (or **`Failed`** if the bank transfer failed to settle).
4. Tick **"I confirm the bank debit has been returned or did not settle and checked its evidence"** and click **Confirm reversal**.

**What happens immediately:**
- A positive reversal entry is added to your **Bank book** on the reversal date, restoring your bank balance.
- The money is returned to the linked **Government receipt(s)** so it can be used again.
- The **Claim** balance goes back to pending so you can pay the beneficiary properly.
- A button called **Create corrected payment** appears at the top of the reversed payment! Click it to open a new payment form pre-filled with the old details, fix whatever was wrong, attach proof, and post the replacement.

### 9.3 Reversing a Posted Government Receipt
If a Government receipt was posted by mistake or a cheque bounced:
1. **First:** If any outward payments were funded by this receipt, you must reverse those outward payments first (a receipt can only be reversed when 100% of its balance is unallocated/available).
2. Open the receipt under **Government receipts** and scroll to **Reverse posted receipt**.
3. Enter the **Reversal date** (must be on or after the receipt's credit date, and on or after any linked payment reversal dates), **Bank reversal evidence reference**, and **Reversal reason** (at least 10 characters).
4. Tick the confirmation checkbox and click **Confirm reversal**.

> **Important Date Rule After a Reversal:**
> When a payment reversal frees up receipt money (or claim balance) on a certain date (say, `15 May`), you cannot reuse that freed-up money for a new payment dated *earlier* than `15 May`. Always date new payments or receipt reversals **on or after** the latest linked payment reversal date.

## 10. Step 6: Checking Your Bank Book & Matching Bank Statements

### 10.1 Viewing Your Bank Book (`Bank book`)
Click **Bank book** in the left menu at any time to see every bank account's:
- **Opening balance** and opening date,
- Every posted **Receipt (+)**, **Payment (−)**, **Reversal (+/-)**, and **Other bank movement (+/-)** in chronological order, and
- The **Running balance** after each transaction.
Clicking any transaction ID in the Bank Book takes you straight to that receipt, payment, or adjustment!

---

### 10.2 Recording Bank Charges & Bank Interest (`Other bank movements`)
Often your real bank statement has small entries that are neither Government receipts nor beneficiary payments—such as **SMS charges, cheque book charges, DD commission, or bank interest**.
To keep your FundTrace Bank Book matching your bank passbook to the exact paise:
1. Click **Other bank movements** in the left menu, then click **Record bank movement**.
2. Select the **Bank account** and enter the **Date** (on or after the bank's opening date).
3. Select the **Category**:
   - `Bank charge` or `Other debit` (deducts money from your Bank Book)
   - `Interest` or `Other credit` (adds money to your Bank Book, **without** creating Government receipt funding)
4. Enter the **Amount (INR)**, **Bank evidence reference**, and **Reason** (at least 10 characters).
5. Tick the confirmation box and click **Post bank movement**.
*(If you ever need to reverse an adjustment, open it and click **Reverse adjustment** at the top-right.)*

---

### 10.3 Importing a Bank Statement (`Bank reconciliation → Import statement`)
To match your Society's records against your official bank statement:

1. Click **Bank reconciliation** in the left menu, then click **Import statement**.
2. Fill in the top details:
   - **Bank account *(Required)*:** Choose the bank account.
   - **Statement start & Statement end *(Required)*:** The first and last date covered by this statement period. *(Note: Statement periods for the same bank must not overlap previous imports—import one month or period after another in sequence.)*
   - **Statement opening balance (INR) & Statement closing balance (INR) *(Required)*:** Enter the exact opening and closing balances printed on your bank statement for this period.
   - **Text date format *(Required)*:** Choose `YYYY-MM-DD` (e.g., `2026-04-15`) or `DD/MM/YYYY` (e.g., `15/04/2026`) to match how dates are written in your CSV/Excel file.
3. **Provide Your Statement Rows (Two Ways):**
   - **Upload a File:** Click **Choose File** under *Statement file* and select your `.csv`, `.xlsx`, or `.xls` file (up to 2 MB and up to 2,000 rows), **OR**
   - **Paste CSV Text:** Type or paste CSV rows into the box (for example, starting with a header line `Date,Reference,Narration,Debit,Credit,Balance`).
   - *Important Requirements for Statement Rows:*
     - Must have a **header row** at the top.
     - Rows must be sorted **from oldest date to newest date** (ascending).
     - Must have **separate Debit and Credit columns** (on any row, leave the unused column blank or `0`).
4. Click **Read statement**.
5. Under **Column mapping**, check that each dropdown (`Date`, `Value date`, `Reference`, `Narration`, `Debit`, `Credit`, `Balance`) points to the right column from your file. (If your Excel file has multiple sheets, pick the right **Worksheet** first.)
6. Click **Preview import**. FundTrace will check all dates, math, and running balances and show you a preview table.
7. Tick the confirmation checkbox and click **Import reviewed statement**.

---

### 10.4 Matching Statement Lines & Recording Exceptions
After importing a statement, you will see all its lines listed with their status (`Unmatched`, `Partially matched`, `Matched`, or `Exception`):

1. Click **Review line** next to any statement row.
2. **To Match It with Your Bank Book:**
   - Under **Match remaining amount**, pick the corresponding transaction from the **Bank movement** dropdown and enter the **Match amount (INR)**.
   - *(If one bank statement entry covers multiple book entries—or vice versa—you can click **Add movement** or match part of the amount.)*
   - If the statement date or reference number is slightly different from what you typed in FundTrace, fill in the **Reason for date/reference difference** box (at least 10 characters).
   - Tick the confirmation checkbox and click **Confirm matches**.
   - *(Made a wrong match? Click **Remove match** next to the linked movement, enter a 10+ character reason, and confirm.)*
3. **To Flag an Unknown Entry as an Exception:**
   - If a statement line is an unknown bank entry or bank error that needs follow-up, scroll to the **Exception** form at the bottom of the line page.
   - Keep `New exception` selected, enter the **Follow-up owner annotation** (name of the person checking with the bank), keep Status as **`Open`**, enter a **Reason** (at least 10 characters), tick confirmation, and click **Save exception**.
   - Once the issue is sorted out and you have recorded/matched the entry, update that Exception's status from `Open` to **`Resolved`**.

---

### 10.5 Locking a Reconciled Period (`Reconciled periods`)
Once a month or quarter is completely reconciled, you can **Lock** it so nobody can accidentally add, reverse, or alter transactions in that period (or any earlier date!):

1. Make sure that for the chosen period:
   - Your imported statement(s) cover the exact start and end dates without gaps,
   - Every single statement line and every Bank Book movement in that period is **`Matched`**,
   - All exceptions in that period are **`Resolved`**, and
   - The statement's opening and closing balances match your Bank Book's opening and closing balances.
2. Click **Reconciled periods** in the left menu.
3. Select the **Bank account**, enter the **Period start** and **Period end** dates, type a **Reason** (at least 10 characters, e.g., *"April 2026 bank reconciliation completed and verified"*), tick confirmation, and click **Lock reconciled period**.
4. **Need to Reopen a Locked Period Later?**
   - Click the **Reopen** button next to the locked period, enter a 10+ character reason, tick confirmation, and click **Confirm reopening**. *(If multiple periods are locked, you must reopen the latest locked period first.)*

## 11. Step 7: Tracking Tax Deductions & Tax Credits Separately

When Government departments pay your Society's bills, they often deduct tax at source (such as Income Tax TDS or GST TDS). That deducted tax is **not cash in your bank account**—it sits as a tax credit with the tax department until your accountant claims or sets it off. FundTrace gives you a dedicated **Tax register** so your tax credits are tracked accurately without ever mixing them up with bank cash.

### 11.1 Step A: Configure Tax Treatments (`Tax treatments`)
FundTrace never guesses tax rates. Before creating a tax record, enter the tax rate confirmed by your Society's accountant:
1. Click **Tax treatments** in the left menu (or click *Configure tax treatments* at the top of the Tax register).
2. Select the **Society**.
3. Enter the **Tax treatment name** (for example, *Income Tax TDS 194C* or *GST TDS*).
4. Enter the **Confirmed rate (%)** (for example, `2` for 2%).
5. Enter the **Effective from** date, **Calculation basis** (e.g., *Gross bill excluding GST*), **Accountant confirmation / evidence reference**, and **Reason** (at least 10 characters).
6. Tick the confirmation box and click **Save tax treatment**.

### 11.2 Step B: Create a Tax Record (`Tax register → New tax record`)
1. Click **Tax register** in the left menu, then click **New tax record**.
2. Select the **Society**, **Project**, **Tax date**, and **Tax treatment**.
3. **Linked receipt *(Optional)*:** If this tax was deducted from a specific Government receipt you already posted, select its Payment ID here.
4. Enter the **Certificate / tax ledger reference** (for example, Form 16A / 26AS / GSTR-7 reference—must be unique in this Society).
5. Enter the **Tax base (INR)** (the bill amount on which tax is calculated).
   - FundTrace will automatically calculate $\text{Base} \times \text{Rate}$. If the actual expected tax is slightly different due to rounding, you can enter an **Expected tax override (INR)** and explain why in the **Override reason** box (at least 10 characters).
6. Enter the **Deducted from bill (INR)** amount, optional **Linked bank amount annotation (INR)** (only if linked to a posted receipt), and choose the **Initial status** (`Expected`, `Deducted`, `Credit Pending`, or `Disputed`).
7. Under **Supporting document (required)**, attach a PDF, PNG, or JPEG proof file (up to 2 MB), tick the confirmation box, and click **Create tax record**.

### 11.3 Step C: Record Tax Credits, Set-Offs & Status Updates
Whenever your accountant checks your tax portal (Form 26AS / GST portal) or files a return:
1. Open the tax record from the **Tax register**.
2. Scroll down to **Record tax event** and choose the **Event type**:
   - **`Credit`:** When the deducted tax officially appears as credited in your tax ledger (cannot exceed the Expected amount; if more was credited, use `Adjustment increase`).
   - **`Set-off`:** When your accountant uses available tax credit to pay tax (cannot exceed your currently **Available tax credit**, and cannot be used while the record is marked `Disputed`).
   - **`Adjustment increase` / `Adjustment decrease`:** For documented accountant adjustments to the credited amount.
   - **`Status`:** To change the status (for example, to mark a record `Disputed` or clear a dispute back to `Deducted`).
3. Enter the **Event date** (must be on or after the tax record date and any earlier events on that record), **Amount (INR)**, **Event evidence reference**, **Reason** (at least 10 characters), and attach your supporting document (PDF/PNG/JPEG, up to 2 MB).
4. Tick the confirmation box and click **Record tax event**.

## 13. Step 9: Keeping Your Records Safe (Backups, Restore & Power Cuts)

Because all your records live on your computer without cloud sync, **taking regular backups onto a USB Pen Drive or External Hard Disk is the most important habit** to protect your Society against computer theft, hard-disk failure, or hardware damage.

### 13.1 How Backups Work (`Backup & recovery`)
1. **Automatic Daily Backups:** Every time you open FundTrace (and once every 24 hours while it stays open), FundTrace automatically creates a verified backup on your computer.
2. **Creating a Manual Backup at the End of Your Workday (Recommended):**
   - Click **Backup & recovery** in the left menu (or **FundTrace → Backup & recovery** in the top menu).
   - Tick the box: **"I understand that backup folders include private records and their encryption key."**
   - Click **Create verified backup**.
   - FundTrace will check every single record, attachment, and encryption key, and display the path to the newly created folder (for example, `...\workspace-backups\backup-2026-11-14T...`).
3. **Copy to a USB Pen Drive / External Drive:**
   - Open that `workspace-backups` folder in Windows File Explorer and **copy the entire dated `backup-...` folder** onto your USB pen drive or external hard drive.
   - **Golden Rule:** Inside each `backup-...` folder are three files (`fundtrace.sqlite`, `encryption.key`, and `manifest.json`). **Always keep all three files together inside their dated folder!** Never copy `fundtrace.sqlite` by itself, because without its matching `encryption.key`, encrypted records cannot be read.

---

### 13.2 How to Restore Your Records from a Backup
If you move to a new computer, or need to restore an earlier backup:

#### In the Windows Desktop App:
1. Click **FundTrace → Restore backup** in the top-left menu bar.
2. Select the **entire dated `backup-...` folder** (for example, from your USB drive or backup folder).
3. A confirmation box will show you the **Backup date** and your **Active workspace** path. Check the date carefully—any entries made *after* that backup date will not be in the backup.
4. Click **Restore backup**.
5. FundTrace will verify the backup, safely keep a copy of your previous workspace folder alongside it (`workspace-before-restore-...`), and automatically restart with your restored records!

#### In the Legacy Folder Version:
1. Close FundTrace (`Ctrl + C` in the black launcher window).
2. Double-click **`Restore FundTrace.cmd`** in the FundTrace folder.
3. Paste the full path to your dated `backup-...` folder and press Enter.
4. Type **`RESTORE`** (in capital letters) and press Enter, then start FundTrace again.

---

### 13.3 What to Do After a Power Cut or Computer Crash
To protect your database from corruption, FundTrace locks your workspace while the app is open so two copies cannot write to it at the same time.
- If your computer suddenly loses power or restarts while FundTrace was open, the lock file might still be sitting there when you turn the computer back on.
- **How to Fix It in 1 Click (Desktop App):** When you open FundTrace after a crash, a message box will say *"Could not open the workspace"*. Simply click the **`Unlock after a crash`** button in that message box! FundTrace will remove the old lock and open right up.
- **How to Fix It (Legacy Folder Version):** Double-click **`Unlock FundTrace.cmd`**, and then double-click **`Start FundTrace.cmd`**.

## 14. Common Questions & Simple Troubleshooting Guide

If FundTrace ever stops an action with a message box, it is protecting your books from an accounting mismatch. Here is what the most common messages mean and how to solve them right away:

| Message Shown on Screen | What It Means in Simple Words | How to Fix It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Credit date must be on or after the bank opening balance date."** *(or Payment date precedes bank opening balance date)* | You entered a receipt or payment date that is *earlier* than the Opening Balance Date of that bank account. | Either correct the receipt/payment date, or (if no transactions have been posted on that bank yet) edit the Bank Account's Opening Balance Date to an earlier date. |
| **"Attach bank evidence before posting."** *(or Attach supporting payment evidence before submission)* | Every finalized receipt and outward payment requires at least one proof file (PDF, PNG, or JPG). | Scroll to the **Evidence** section on that page, choose your file (up to 2 MB), and click **Attach evidence** before posting/submitting. |
| **"Receipt allocations must equal the bank debit."** | On an Outward Payment, the total amount in **Claim distributions** does not equal the total amount in **Receipt funding**. | Check the rupee amounts in both sections so their totals match to the exact paise. |
| **"Allocation exceeds the available receipt balance."** | You are trying to take more money from a Government receipt than it currently has left. | Choose another posted receipt from the same project and bank account, or split the amount across two receipts using **Add receipt row**. |
| **"Payment exceeds the remaining claim amount."** | You are trying to pay a beneficiary more than the unpaid (**Pending**) balance left on their approved Claim. | Reduce the payment amount to match the claim's pending balance, or create/approve a new claim if there is an additional bill. |
| **"This movement would overdraw the bank book on its date or a later date."** | Recording this payment (or reversal) on the chosen date would cause your Bank Book balance to drop below ₹0 (negative) on that date or on a subsequent day. | Check the **Bank book** to see your running balance on that date. Make sure the funding receipt is posted and dated on or before the payment date. |
| **"Reverse all linked payments before reversing this receipt."** | You tried to reverse a Government receipt whose money has already been used to pay beneficiaries. | Open the receipt (or Payment ID 360) to see which outward payments used its funds, reverse those payments first, and then reverse the receipt. |
| **"Use a date on or after the latest linked payment reversal; released funding cannot be backdated."** | Money freed up by reversing a payment on a certain date cannot be spent (or reversed) on an earlier date. | Set your new payment date (or receipt reversal date) to be **on or after** the date the earlier payment was reversed. |
| **"This bank period is locked. Reopen it with a reason before changing this date or an earlier balance."** | You tried to post or reverse an entry inside (or before) a bank period that was locked under **Reconciled periods**. | Check if your transaction date has a typo. If you genuinely need to change a locked period, go to **Reconciled periods** and click **Reopen** on the locked period first. |
| **"Bank opening balance, date and account number are locked once financial history exists."** | Once a bank account has a posted receipt, statement, or adjustment, its opening balance and account number cannot be changed. | You can still edit the bank's nickname, branch, or IFSC. If a new bank account was opened, add it as a new Bank Account record. |
